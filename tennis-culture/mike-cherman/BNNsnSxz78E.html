Cleaned interview

Complete chronological reading notes for this recording. The wording is edited and paraphrased for clarity; timestamps open the source and these paragraphs should not be quoted as Mike’s exact words.

  1. 00:00:00

    Begin with one experiment, not a full line

    Mike argues against starting with a large clothing collection. Making one shirt, encountering mistakes, and repeating the process teaches more than presenting an imaginary finished brand.

  2. 00:00:29

    Choose an internship for responsibility

    A famous company name does not guarantee useful attention. Mike recommends working with an individual or small team willing to teach in exchange for real help, because responsibility creates a faster learning loop.

  3. 00:00:57

    Prepare the file for the cutter

    He lays out arched and script graphics, mirrors them because the vinyl is cut from the reverse side, saves the files, and sends the design from a computer to the cutting machine.

  4. 00:01:25

    Weed and heat-press the transfer

    After the cutter traces the design, excess vinyl is removed by hand. The remaining graphic is positioned on the shirt, pressed with heat, and peeled from its carrier to reveal the sample.

  5. 00:01:53

    Sampling is the threshold

    Mike’s final instruction is concrete: decide what to make, produce the sample, and put it in front of someone. A product becomes learnable only after execution.
