Full interview, in order

  1. 00:00:14

    ICNY began as a solution to Mike’s commute

    Cycling daily between Brooklyn and Manhattan exposed Mike to repeated collisions and near misses. Because he preferred dark clothing, he needed visibility that did not feel like adding a separate safety uniform.

  2. 00:00:44

    Moving legs make reflective socks conspicuous

    After a particularly bad crash, he designed reflective socks. A cyclist’s legs repeatedly cross a driver’s light beam, creating a flashing motion that can be noticed more readily than one static reflective patch.

  3. 00:01:12

    A stranger supplied the first proof

    A driver told Mike at a stoplight that the socks were visible from far away. That unsolicited reaction suggested the personal solution could serve other cyclists, runners, commuters, and pedestrians.

  4. 00:01:41

    Function is integrated into daily clothing

    ICNY tries to make safety compatible with ordinary menswear. The shown collections combine reflective construction with heat retention, waterproofing, moisture management, technical fabrics, and layered ventilation.

  5. 00:02:09

    Technical detail advances by season

    The spring range adds spacer fabrics, custom necklines, and double layers whose inner mesh helps move moisture and heat. Mike frames each material choice as part of joining performance with lifestyle.

  6. 00:02:37

    Agenda is relationship infrastructure

    The trade show gives the brand direct access to unfamiliar buyers, possible collaborators, and other operators. Mike points to a Toronto conversation as the kind of connection that probably would not happen without sharing the physical event.
