Philippines — planning the distribution trip.

Why the Philippines is not arbitrary: Mike already has an Alex Eala shirt, and Tennis T‑Shirts already ran a Filipino-fighter capsule by @patpalacio — the Filipino thread exists here and this extends it. Jerry and Tom also need an editor and want to go regardless, so the trip and the proof are one trip.

Where Eala actually comes from: she started as a preschooler on the shell courts of Valle Verde Country Club in Pasig, first coached by her grandfather Roberto “Bobby” Maniego, before leaving at 13 for the Rafa Nadal Academy. Shell courts — crushed-shell surface — are the distinctly Filipino ground; a giveaway shot on shell says “here,” not “anywhere.” Valle Verde is a private club, so it is an ask-permission lead, not a walk-up.

The courts, from legendary down to walk-up — each checked 19 August 2026, none contacted yet:

  • Rizal Memorial Tennis Center, Malate, Manila — the country’s historic tennis ground, renovated for the January 2026 Philippine Women’s Open, the WTA 125 that brought Eala home. Its Felicisimo Ampon courts are publicly bookable through the Philippine Sports Commission at ₱600 an hour daytime, ₱1,000 evenings. Giving out Eala shirts at the venue of her homecoming tournament is the strongest possible frame, and it is bookable by anyone.
  • UP Diliman tennis courts, Quezon City — three hard and three shell courts behind the university track oval. The university mirror of the UCLA giveaway: same motion, same crowd, one ocean over.
  • Rancho Uno Tennis Club, Marikina — ₱100 an hour, open to non-members, reservations and coaches reached through Facebook, hosts its own tennis meets. The purest grassroots entry in Metro Manila.
  • MWSS Tennis Club, Balara, Quezon City — a club that meets on two outdoor shell courts on Katipunan Road. Shell again; community again.
  • PCA courts, Paco, Manila — the Philippine Columbian Association’s courts on Quirino Avenue Extension, one of the oldest tennis addresses in the city, known for a friendly mixed crowd.
  • Cebu — Asmara Tennis Association, clay courts with hitting partners around ₱200–300 an hour; and Villa Aurora Tennis Club, two dirt-clay community courts with no advertising, found through Facebook.

Facebook is the platform. Philippine courts and clubs organize on Facebook, not Instagram — Rancho Uno takes bookings there, Villa Aurora only exists there, and coach listings run through pages and groups. Before the trip: join the Metro Manila and Cebu tennis groups, message the specific court pages, and confirm every handle live — none of the above has been contacted, and unverified handles stay unverified until a reply exists.

Getting there, for this trip specifically.

  • Los Angeles to Manila: Philippine Airlines flies nonstop LAX–MNL, about 15 hours 40 minutes, 17 times a week. Round trips have recently priced roughly $680–1,160 depending on the week (checked 19 August 2026); one-stops via Taipei, Tokyo, or Seoul usually undercut the nonstop. Live fares.
  • When: November to early December. Typhoon season tapers by November, and from mid-December seats and fares spike as overseas Filipinos fly home for Christmas — book before that wave, and prefer Monday or Tuesday departures, which price lowest on this route.
  • Manila to Cebu: about an hour and a quarter on Cebu Pacific or Philippine Airlines; cheap if booked ahead. Grab covers ground travel in both cities; a Globe or Smart eSIM covers data from landing.
  • The five carry-ons, honestly: boxes of identical branded shirts read to customs as commercial importation, not luggage. Keep the count gift-scale, split it across travelers, and declare honestly — or the sponsorship conversation with Mike’s side has to include who handles importation.
  • The Delta standby leads, recorded once and not argued: Andrey Frazao, Van (both former roommates of Jerry’s), and Bianca each have a Delta standby pass, reportedly found through someone selling one via a music connection; the plan on the list was to ask whether any Delta employee wants to sell one. Paid transfer of a buddy or standby pass normally breaches employee-travel terms, and the consequence lands on the employee, not the buyer. A checked search of Craigslist and Facebook Marketplace on 18 August found zero real listings — such passes trade privately if at all. Spellings are from dictation and unverified.
